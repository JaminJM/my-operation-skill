Vertical myth-busting infographic card for a Chinese education advisory article about Hong Kong IANG. The image should clearly communicate that IANG is often misunderstood. Create one title area at the top and four separate misconception cards below it. Card 1 represents "IANG does not guarantee staying in Hong Kong" with a cautious pathway or checkmark-with-caveat icon. Card 2 represents "IANG is not permanent residency" with an ID card and residence icon. Card 3 represents "applying later is not always better" with a calendar and clock icon emphasizing timing. Clean editorial infographic style, professional Hong Kong study-abroad consultancy brand, teal background with cream card panels and warm yellow highlights, flat vector illustration, simple line icons, high contrast, easy to read on mobile, no official logos, no fake government documents, no fear-based imagery, no clutter.
