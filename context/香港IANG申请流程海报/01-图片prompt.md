# 香港 IANG 申请流程海报 Prompt

### 图 1：香港 IANG 申请流程竖版海报

用途：
用于公众号、小红书或咨询朋友圈发布的 IANG 申请流程说明海报，帮助香港非本地毕业生快速理解申请路径。

位置：
独立流程海报。

比例：
9:16 竖版海报。

是否包含文字：
包含中文标题和每一步短文案。要求模型直接渲染中文，后期可在 Canva 中检查和修改。

Prompt:
Create a clean editorial infographic poster about the Hong Kong Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG) application process, aspect ratio 9:16 vertical poster. The poster should look like a professional Hong Kong study-abroad advisory visual, not an advertisement. Use a modern, trustworthy education-consulting style with a calm but not single-color palette: deep teal, warm coral accents, light neutral background, and small Hong Kong skyline or map-line details as subtle context. 

Layout structure: a vertical step-by-step flow with 7 numbered stages connected by a clear line path from top to bottom. At the top, render the Chinese title "香港 IANG 申请流程" and subtitle "毕业后留港工作，先看清你属于哪一类". Under the title, include a small split decision block showing two applicant categories with Chinese text: "毕业 6 个月内：一般无需先有工作 offer" and "毕业超过 6 个月：一般需要已获得香港聘用". Do not use official Hong Kong government logos, university logos, or immigration department logos.

The 7 main flow stages must be visually represented with these exact Chinese step labels and short copy:
1. "确认申请资格" - "非本地毕业生 / 合资格大湾区校园毕业生"
2. "判断申请类别" - "近期毕业生或非近期毕业生"
3. "准备申请材料" - "证件、学历证明、照片、聘用文件"
4. "网上递交申请" - "上传文件并缴付申请费"
5. "等待入境处审批" - "近期约 2 周；非近期约 4 周"
6. "获批后下载 e-Visa" - "缴付签证签发费后下载或打印"
7. "开始留港工作" - "首次逗留通常为 24 个月"

Visual elements: use minimal line icons for graduation cap, calendar, checklist, laptop upload, clock, payment card, QR-code style e-Visa, and Hong Kong workplace building. Use clear hierarchy, generous spacing, thin connector lines, and small warning callout boxes. Add one bottom caution strip and render the Chinese text "具体要求以香港入境处最新规定及个人情况为准". Make the poster easy to scan on mobile.

Text handling: render the Chinese title, subtitle, category labels, seven step labels, short step copy, and bottom caution note directly inside the poster. Keep every Chinese text block short, large, and readable on mobile. Avoid tiny dense text. Leave enough margin around every Chinese text block so the user can later edit it in Canva if needed. All important semantic content must appear inside the image, not only as visual placeholders.

Negative instructions: no fake official seals, no government or university logos, no dense legal text, no exaggerated success guarantee, no visa approval promise, no stock-photo people, no cartoon characters, no cluttered background, no tiny unreadable text, no garbled Chinese characters, no misspelled Chinese, no dark dramatic mood, no generic travel poster style.

中文设计备注：
图片中应直接渲染以下中文文案，后期可在 Canva 中检查和修改：

- 标题：香港 IANG 申请流程
- 副标题：毕业后留港工作，先看清你属于哪一类
- 判断 1：毕业 6 个月内：一般申请时无需先有工作 offer
- 判断 2：毕业超过 6 个月：一般需要已获得香港聘用
- 步骤 1：确认申请资格
- 步骤 2：判断近期/非近期毕业生

- 步骤 4：网上递交并缴费
- 步骤 5：等待入境处审批
